Healthcare Providers Scope of Practice Amendments
Senator Evan J. Vickers proposes the following amendments:

Section 2:

20 →requires that the office create a report that details the office's recommendation following a

22 →requires that the office provide the report to the Business and Labor Interim Committee;
→requires that the Business and Labor Interim Committee take certain actions after
receiving a report from the office;
23 →requires that the office create a final report after incorporating any changes from the
24 Business and Labor Interim Committee;
25 →provides the criteria the office shall consider when conducting a review; and

Section 3:
2. Line 158 through 169:
158 (5) The office shall provide the preliminary report described in Subsection (4) to the
159 Business and Labor Interim Committee in accordance with Section 13-1b-304.
160 (6) Upon receiving the preliminary report under Subsection (5), the Business and Labor
161 Interim Committee {may} shall:
162 (a) recommend draft legislation to address the preliminary report described in
163 Subsection (4);
164 (b) recommend that the Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing make rules
163 Subsection (4);
164 (b) recommend that the Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing make rules
165 in accordance with Title 63G, Chapter 3, Utah Administrative Rulemaking Act; or
166 (c) take any other action that the Business and Labor Interim Committee deems appropriate.
167 (7) After presenting the preliminary report, the office shall create and publish a final report
168 based on the preliminary report and any recommendations provided by the Business and
169 Labor Interim Committee.
